## Keep learning separate from established practice

This is a personal learning plan. A topic being listed here does not mean you lack the skill, and marking it Learned does not establish production readiness. The initial topics are suggested exercises based on the manual's existing projects; edit or remove them to reflect your own priorities.

## Use three simple states

**Next** means you intend to focus on the topic. Keep that list to roughly three–five priorities. **Practicing** means you are actively working through an exercise. **Learned** records your own assessment after meeting your completion criteria; it is not an automated competency check or certification.

Each topic has a reason, a practical exercise, completion criteria and an optional related guide. An exercise should produce something you can explain or inspect: a synthetic test result, a design decision, a small implementation or a recovery walkthrough.

## Make the exercise safe and concrete

Use disposable environments and synthetic data. A cloud exercise here does not provision infrastructure or authorize changes to another project. Record actual checks where the application keeps its evidence, and avoid putting credentials, clinical records or private links into a personal learning note.

## Save and export your plan

Edits and status changes are stored in this browser when storage is available. There is no account or cross-device sync. With blocked storage, changes last only while this page stays open. Export the roadmap before clearing browser data if you want a portable copy.

The export identifies statuses as self-reported and says that no exercises or application checks were executed by the handbook. The roadmap never changes the review status of source-based chapters or marks a planned Lumen AWS deployment as complete.
